Featured project

Student Academic Intelligence System

Smarter academic monitoring through data, automation and intelligent insights.

Problem

Academic information such as attendance, curriculum progress, student activities and performance data is often managed through separate, disconnected processes — making it difficult for students and faculty to monitor academic progress and identify potential risks early.

Solution

The Student Academic Intelligence System is a centralized academic management and intelligence platform. It combines attendance tracking, academic activity monitoring, risk awareness, QR-based attendance, dashboards and analytics into one digital environment.
